I'm a demographer with seven years of experience translating complex population data into findings that inform policy, planning, and advocacy. My work at the Population Reference Bureau spanned enrollment and education data, mortality research, migration analysis, child health indicators, and population projections.

I've worked extensively with California Department of Education data, AMBAG and SANDAG population projections, U.S. Census Bureau microdata, and a range of federal, state, and local sources. Proficient in R, SAS, GIS, and statistical methods from regression to cohort-component projection.

Currently open to full-time roles in demographic research, enrollment planning, and population data analysis.
