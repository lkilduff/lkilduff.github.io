About

I'm a quantitative social scientist with 7 years of experience in demographic data analysis and a master's in Demography from UC Berkeley. I take large, messy datasets and turn them into actionable insights that drive decisions. I specialize in U.S. Census Bureau data, administrative data, and CDC data — and am fluent in data best practices and privacy — translating complex population trends into clear, compelling stories for senior audiences.
